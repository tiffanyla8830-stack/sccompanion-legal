Both are independent community projects. Neither is operated by us, and neither

**Data accuracy.** Commodity prices, stock levels and trade routes come from
player reports and are updated periodically, not in real time. They may be
inaccurate, incomplete or out of date. Ship statistics come from game files and
may lag behind the current live patch. **Do not rely on the Bot's output as the
sole basis for in-game decisions.** Verify prices and stock in game before
committing credits or cargo.
